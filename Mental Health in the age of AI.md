An engineer's purpose is to think deeply and critically, make sure that they are asking the right questions, and that they are leading the product in the right direction.

In the new world of working all of the easy work has gone to AI, things like coding or writing docs. It use to be that there was a natural mix of a workday to provide between hard tasks and routine tasks. This mix would naturally give the mind a break, and allow engineers a reprieve from just always thinking or always working hard.

All that is left now is high-exertion work: judgement, design, critique, figuring out what matters. This makes burnout so much faster and much easier than it use to.

Variety doesn't happen on its own anymore, so we now have to make time to do it on purpose when it use to come for free. The day should be about optimizing for clarity of thinking, and slowing down the need to keep being performant - let the agents perform while you rest. Give the mind room to wander, and not always be on, doodle, go for a walk, etc. The agent is still running, you don't have to be.

This isn't just some free perk that comes with AI, this is an actual critical thing that needs to be done in order for an engineer to maintain the _quality_ of their work. The best thing you can bring to your job is a clear mind.
