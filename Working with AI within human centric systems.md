What ultimately makes modern systems work in the age of AI is finding that human element within each system, and identifying that "checkpoint" spot where people are required to think about decisions that actually matter. A sort of reframing and thinking about the expectation and the goal of the system.

An example is [[PR Descriptions]] and how those are a human to human system, and therefore for the most part should maintain that human checkpoint to _know_ what is being put into the code base and to _share_ context across the team. That said, not all PRs really need a human, and we should value the time of coworkers taking time out of their days to review PRs and not require them for purely mechanical things that could potentially be auto approved.

It's a balance.
