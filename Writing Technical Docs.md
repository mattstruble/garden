It's easy to forget when writing a doc the inherent biases that come with being a native english speaker, and the assumptions that are made. When working on a large team it's important to ensure that things are written clearly and succinctly without introducing new terminology, or using words with double meanings. 

I think [[ASD-STE100]] is a good step towards this, albeit maybe a bit too extreme.

When dealing with work that is heavily in your domain of knowledge, sometimes even a single sentence can hold so much information/context/pretense that a reader cannot understand and will just glaze over - and set the mood for the rest of the document. How do you impart that domain knowledge to make it easier to onboard people into a new domain?
