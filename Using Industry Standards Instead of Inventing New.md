Inventing new things is exciting, you feel as though you are making your mark on the world, and the team gets to share in on that cohesion and excitement of a shared understanding having worked on the New Thing together.

However, by taking an approach that already uses a defined industry standard, that opens up even further opportunities. The team is required to ask each other questions, and therefore learn from each other, instead of just writing something from scratch. In turn you gain value from these other companies work, and are given the opportunity to drive impact, shape solutions, and give back to the community instead of remaining insular.

In doing so a company/team can become thought leaders, and contribute back to the industry they work in and help shape how it grows.
