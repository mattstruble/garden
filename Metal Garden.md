A vast and expansive game where you play as a character within a massive mega structure. Everything within it is explained via overhearing character conversations, or the occasional left behind note. 

It feels massive and expansive, and does an incredible job with these huge landscapes and making yourself feel infinitely small. 

It has a weird mix of organic, and inorganic material, and some of the inorganic took on organic life. 

It left me genuinely curious for the world and just completely enthralled with every moment. I couldn't figure out screenshots on steamdeck when connected with a remote controller, but I just had to take photos of some really breathtaking scenes. Genuinely made me stop to appreciate everything in the world in moments of [[Quiet Observations]]. 

> Finally preparing to leave the elevator, and facing an ever stranger landscape, you nonetheless find the familiar step of your nomadic wandering returning: the promise engraved in every mile traveled that the seeking itself is the answer.
