[[Software Engineering]] and [[Software Architecture]] have changed so drastically over the last 7 months. My days are spent just deciding on things. Writing RFCs, ADRs, Specs, deciding what the architecture will look like, understanding all the components. Then when it comes to implementing the code it's making decisions on what the LLM is trying to do. 

Previously an engineer would do some design work, and then you could enter a flow state of coding. This ebb and flow would give you a reprieve from making decisions and just code. That doesn't exist anymore. 

You can't trust the LLM output because it doesn't have the correct knowledge (even frontier Claude opus 5.5), and needs correction or adjustments or alignments to keep it on task. Otherwise you're left with bloat. 

A lot of days after work I reach home absolutely mentally drained and fatigued and unable to make any more decisions for the day.
