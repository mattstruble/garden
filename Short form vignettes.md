[[Metal Garden]] and [[Mika and the Witch's Mountain]] got me thinking about games which are small and concise. A little vignette that you can quickly step in/out of and just experience an entire package. 

There is no waiting around for slow introductions, nor fumbling between different stages. These games are tight in their mechanics and story telling, and manage to pack so much into such a small package. Nothing feels like it overstays its welcome, and most importantly, it doesn't try to cram in a bunch of systems to stretch the mechanics across a multi-hour session. Everything has a purpose.

The world almost feels richer and more engaging. You're able to experience it all within a few short hours, so it stays in memory and feels like a more significant impact than stretching it out across multiple sittings. With life happening it's so easy to forget what happens in larger games, and I think that detracts from their impact.
