I was playing through this game with my gaming group for a night every odd week over the past month or so. After completing it last night it was one of the most surreal and oddly intimate gaming experiences I think I've had the pleasure to be a part of?

The game's main feature is a proximity based microphone to talk to your party member sand to solve puzzles together as you walk around and explore this open world. As you progress the puzzles naturally find ways to kind of interrupt that communication ability and all you're left with is movements and different ways to position your arms to try and communicate over large distances, or through sound proof barriers. However, there is always time to kind of explore puzzles beforehand together and come up with a plan of how to communicate with the limitations in order to solve it.

That is until the finale. The final 10 puzzles completely remove the microphones (thematically sound is being drowned out by a ringing bell), and also drains the players of their custom colors and therefore you have no way of identifying who is who anymore, the only thing that gave us away was our individual mannerisms in how we moved/interacted with the world that we developed over the course of the game.

What was left was an incredibly intimate experience where we couldn't talk, all we could do was point and gesture, and trying to solve 10 increasingly difficult puzzles together. I don't think I've laughed so hard as I have, nor gotten so frustrated at points. But there's nothing you can do but continue to point at something and gesture wildly in hopes that everything works out in the end.

Then the send off where we were randomly selected to walk into the void waving goodbye to each other at the end of the game.

It was oddly poignant and beautiful, and just an overall remarkable experience.
