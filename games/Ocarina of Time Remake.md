It's kind of beautiful how a game can come out after 20 years and still capture all the nostalgia that I felt as a kid. I don't think I have played this game much since college, so probably around 2011, but up until that time I would play it pretty consistently and basically knew the whole game like the back of my hand. 

So when coming to replay the remake for the Switch 2 it immediately invoked that same child (/ young adult) wonder in me and instantly took me back. I remembered so much of the game, and it all felt so natural and I could just instinctively set up all the puzzles and solve everything in one go. 

I think by the time I finished the main story I had done all the side plots/quests and was only missing three heart pieces and 10 gold skulltelas. Which is kind of crazy how much the mind can hold onto. 

It makes you realize that impactful events still have influence over your life in the present, and things that shape you will always carry with you. I may not have been actively thinking about OoT much in the last 15 years, but it is still a major part of me - and the game that I attribute the most to falling in love with gaming and shaping my entire school/career trajectory. 

So to go back and be able to replay it, re experience it, and just have it feel like riding an old bike again - something you never truly forgot. Was a beautiful experience, and I am grateful that Nintendo was able to capture that feeling and give it the representation it needs.
