A very cute short game with some incredible natural progression. Each day being able to reach higher, glide further, and build bigger relationships with the townspeople was truly rewarding. 

I didn't care much for the underground dungeon areas, especially not that they reset if you left to do the more fun part (in my opinion) of exploring the overworld. 

Catching a breeze and just zooming around, soaring off of high places, and that first time jumping off the mountain and watching the haze and fog slowly peel back to reveal the town and world below made it feel so grand. 

I'm sure there are more things to uncover in the game, but upon reaching the end I was pretty over the whole ordeal after about 5 hours.
