With [[LLM decision fatigue]] and the future of [[Software Architecture]] the working order is becoming increasingly about just designing. Which leads to an awkward state where all the engineers on a team are essentially designing simultaneously on things that are loosely related to each other, so there's a lot of stepping on toes and the flow of decisions is moving fast. 

A design decision by a spec author two days ago influences the design of someone else's spec, and you don't realize the flaw until maybe even later on, maybe 5-6 specs later where you've painted yourself into a corner.
