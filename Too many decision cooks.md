With [[LLM decision fatigue]] and the future of [[Software Architecture]] the working order is becoming increasingly about just designing. Which leads to an awkward state where all the engineers on a team are essentially designing simultaneously on things that are loosely related to each other, so there's a lot of stepping on toes and the flow of decisions is moving fast. Before people would take turns, while you're designing someone else is coding their design, and vice versa, but now we're all just designing.

A design decision by a spec author two days ago influences the design of someone else's spec, and you don't realize the flaw until maybe even later on, maybe 5-6 specs later where you've painted yourself into a corner. 

By constantly having multiple people work on adjacent things, those things are constantly changing as you walk and there is always an imperfect flow of information.
